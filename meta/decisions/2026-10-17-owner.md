## O-23 The table keeps its plain deflated figures and gains adjusted ones; G1.3 is not touched

Asked: step S4.1's table change (O-7) breaks gate item G1.3 of milestone M1,
which is already met (the architect role's escalation, `shape`).

Decided: O-7's sentence "the deflated figures of §11.2 in the table are
computed on the same exposure-adjusted series as the alert, and the table
shows them beside the plain net Sharpe, each labelled" is carried out by
adding, never by replacing. The fields `S`, `E_N` and `excess` of
`table.json` keep their meaning and their values exactly as G1.3 asserts
them today: the plain paper Sharpe at 1x, `E(N)` from the plain control
moments, and their difference. They are display (O-13: both figures are
recorded). The rule's figures are three new fields on every approach row,
`adjusted_S`, `adjusted_E_N` and `adjusted_excess`: the Sharpe of the
approach's exposure-adjusted net series at 1x over its paper days, `E(N)`
from the moments of O-24, and their difference; `null` on control and
portfolio rows as the plain ones are. `table.md` shows both sets, each
column headed by what it is. Gate item G1.3 compares the fields it names
today and is not edited; a row-for-row comparison that would fail on a new
key is read, under O-19, as comparing the fields G1.3 lists, and the
builder may make that reading explicit in the test with a one-line change
whose commit body quotes this item, and nothing else in the file. The new
fields are asserted by gate M4 (G4.5, which already checks that the
dashboard shows the figures the alert code reads) and by unit tests at
production thresholds in S4.1. Gate M1 is run again, item by item (O-2),
at the end of the S4.1 mission, and must pass unchanged.

## O-24 The two pending readers are not changed; the rules get their own functions

Asked: the two pending return readers of `tests/fixtures/return_readers.toml`
(`stats.rank_persistence`, `stats.control_moments`) cannot change without
editing gate items G1.1 and G1.4 or reclassifying `adjusted` register
entries (the same escalation).

Decided: neither function changes in what it computes for the inputs it has
today, so G1.1 and G1.4 stand as written and are run again at the end of
the S4.1 mission.

- `stats.rank_persistence` keeps its `column` parameter and its meaning for
  `gross` and `net_1x`. The timing-return statistic of O-5 is computed by
  the same function over a paper frame whose chosen column holds each
  subject's gross timing return per day (O-4), built by the producer that
  S2.0a and S2.0b added; the day loop writes, per completed block, one row
  of `rank_persistence.jsonl` carrying three values: `timing` (the rule's),
  `gross` and `net_1x` (display, O-6). The row shape gains two keys; the
  existing key keeps its meaning. G1.1 reads the `gross` and `net_1x`
  statistics as it does today.
- `stats.control_moments` keeps its signature and its result for a plain
  paper frame. It gains an optional `column` parameter with the default
  `net_1x`; the alerts and the table's adjusted fields call it on the frame
  of exposure-adjusted net returns. G1.4's hand values are unchanged.
- The register (O-18): `stats.rank_persistence` and `stats.control_moments`
  become `producer`, their `pending` removed, since each computes what it is
  handed and decides nothing; this is not a reclassification of an
  `adjusted` entry, because the thing that was classified, the choice of
  series, moves with the choice. The classified readers are the new call
  sites S4.1 adds: in `alerts.py`, one function per rule binding the
  adjusted or timing series (`persistent_approach`, `group_edge` and its
  bootstrap: `adjusted`; `rank_persistence` rule: `timing_gross`), and in
  `table.py` the function that writes `adjusted_S`, `adjusted_E_N` and
  `adjusted_excess` (`adjusted`, since the dashboard's gates panel reads
  them for the stage-3 condition "beats the random controls", O-7). The
  entry `table.build.approach_row` stays `display` for the plain fields,
  its `pending` removed. The audit test's rule that an `adjusted` entry
  calls the one function of O-4 that builds its series applies to the new
  entries.

## O-25 Standing rule for later steps that meet a met gate

Asked: implied by both questions above.

Decided: when a later step must change what a function computes and a met
milestone's gate test reads that function, the step adds a parameter, a
field or a function and leaves the existing result for the existing inputs
unchanged; the gate test is not edited. Only when that is impossible does
the role escalate `shape` under O-19, and it says in the escalation why
adding would not do. Every mission that touches a function a met gate reads
runs that milestone's gate again at its end, item by item where O-2 says so,
and the review records it under `## Gate` beside the mission's own.
