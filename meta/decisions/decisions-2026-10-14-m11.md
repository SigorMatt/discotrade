84. **Decision 81 (d) made concrete: the derived exposure records are
    stored; REVIEW-10 note 3.** From this step the adjusted series has
    readers that run every day (dormancy, return, the table over a whole
    paper record), and computing it on call reads every journal day of the
    window again for each subject on each day, which a one-year replay
    cannot afford (O-3). So the day stores, under `paper/<track>/`, per
    instrument and trading date `R_i(D)`, and per subject, instrument and
    trading date the journal row count and the summed `position`; the file
    names and layout are the builder's. They are derived, staged with the
    day, written by a new day only, not rewritten by a re-run (I-6),
    rebuilt from the journal and the stored bars to the same content hash,
    and healed by decision 78's rule. `paper.adjusted` reads them, and
    returns on every case of `tests/test_exposure.py` what it returns
    today (the existing tests of that file stay unchanged in what they
    assert); `hbar_i(W)` is the summed position over `W` divided by the
    row count over `W`, 0 with no row. A stored `R_i(D)` equals the value
    computed on call from a later state, after a later roll of the
    instrument (prices adjusted as of `u`, decision 81 (b)). The private
    call `journal._empty()` in `paper.py` is replaced by a public name, and
    a caller that passes staged paper records also passes `overlay`, so
    the staged day's own rows are read (REVIEW-10 note 3).
