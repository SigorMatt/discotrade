115. **DESIGN §13.1: the W profile, version `p1`.** `workers/profile.py`
     computes, for a track, a trading day `D` and a cell, a JSON-ready
     document from W's bars of `D` (DESIGN §4.1) and nothing else. (a)
     Returns: for consecutive stored bars `s-1`, `s` both in W,
     `r(s) = (Ca(s) - Ca(s-1)) / Cr(s-1)`, closes adjusted as of W's last
     bar (DESIGN §5.6), `Cr` raw; "in basis points" is `r * 10000`. Hours
     and weekdays are the bar's open time in its calendar's time zone, as
     §6.2 gives them to a strategy. (b) The common block, per instrument:
     the count of returns; mean, sample deviation (ddof 1) and the
     quantiles 0.01, 0.05, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.95 and 0.99 (numpy's default
     interpolation), in basis points; skewness `m3 / m2**1.5` and excess
     kurtosis `m4 / m2**2 - 3` from central moments; the sample deviation of
     returns by hour of day (0 to 23) and by weekday (0 to 6), in basis
     points; the autocorrelation of returns at lags 1 to 48 (Pearson,
     over the series of W's returns); the share of W's volume by hour of
     day; and, with `rv(s)` the sample deviation of the 24 returns ending at
     `s` (defined from W's 24th return), high regime `rv > ` its 75th
     percentile over W and low regime `rv < ` its 25th, the share of
     defined bars in each and the mean length of their maximal runs. (c) The
     cell block, by the cell's family, per instrument: `trend`: for k in 6,
     24 and 48 bars, the non-overlapping consecutive k-bar sums of returns
     from W's first return: the share of consecutive pairs of the same
     sign (a zero is not of either sign), their lag-1 autocorrelation and
     the count of pairs. `mean_reversion`: events are returns with `|r|`
     above twice W's deviation; for k in 1, 6 and 24, the sum of the k
     returns after an event (events without k later returns in W dropped):
     the share of opposite sign to the event, the mean of that sum times
     minus the event's sign in basis points, and the count. `breakout`: for
     n in 24 and 48, a bar whose close is above the highest (below the
     lowest) of the n closes before it is an up (down) breakout: the share
     of bars with n closes before them that break out; for k in 1, 6 and 24
     the share of the following k-bar sums in the breakout's direction and
     their mean in that direction in basis points; and the mean of (highest
     high minus lowest low over the n bars ending at a bar) over that bar's
     close, in basis points. `volatility_regime`: per regime of (b), the
     mean and deviation of returns in basis points, the lag-1
     autocorrelation over pairs both in the regime, and the count.
     `volume`: events are bars whose volume is more than 2 sample
     deviations above the mean of the 24 volumes before it; the share of
     events; the mean `|r|` of the bar after an event over the mean `|r|` of
     W; for k in 1 and 6 the share of following k-bar sums with the sign of
     the event bar's return and their mean in that direction in basis
     points. `time_of_day`: by hour of day and by weekday, the mean return
     in basis points, the share positive, and the count. (d) A statistic
     with too few observations to be defined is `null` beside its count;
     nothing is filled in. The document carries `"version": "p1"`, the
     instruments in the track's order under their symbols (anonymization,
     item 116, replaces them), the common blocks and the cell block; it
     holds no date, no timestamp, no price, no volume and no bar index of
     the store. Numbers are not rounded here (item 117 (b) renders them).
     (e) Tests, on `test_synth` and on `test_mixed` (the rolling and the
     proportional-fee instruments) with every seed and date in the file:
     the profile for `D` is identical whether the store ends at W's last bar
     or holds T's and later bars (W only, I-3); prices multiplied by one
     constant and volumes by another give the same profile to a relative
     1e-9 (no price or volume unit); two cells of the same family give the
     same document and cells of different families differ only in the cell
     block; at least one figure of each block equals a value the test
     computes by hand from the stored bars without calling the module.
     Changing a definition here changes the version string.

116. **DESIGN §13.2 and §13.3: anonymization and the registry.** (a)
     `workers/anonymize.py`: the generator for a call is
     `numpy.random.default_rng` seeded with the first 8 bytes, big-endian,
     of the sha256 of the call id's UTF-8 text (never Python's `hash`). It
     shuffles the track's instruments and labels them `A`, `B`, ... in the
     shuffled order (a track of more than 26 instruments is refused); every
     instrument-keyed part of a prompt (the profile's blocks, the cost
     lines of item 117) uses that one mapping, in label order. The fields in
     price or volume units are a module constant, empty for `p1`; the
     scaling function multiplies each listed field by a constant drawn per
     instrument from the same generator, uniform on [0.5, 2.0], and is
     tested on its own with a list the test supplies (returns unchanged, a
     price-unit field scaled). Hours of day and weekdays are kept. (b)
     `workers/registry.py`: for a day `D` and a cell, one line per approach
     live or dormant on `D` (as `lifecycle` decides states) whose cell has
     the same family or the same horizon as the cell, the cell itself
     included, ordered by cell index then id: `<id> | <family>/<horizon> |
     <state> | <description>`. No score, return, rank, date, model or count
     appears. A description is sanitized by one function: characters
     outside printable ASCII (0x20 to 0x7E), backticks and angle brackets
     removed, line breaks and runs of whitespace made one space, the
     result stripped and cut to `description_max_chars` (read from
     `config/thresholds.toml`, I-10). The block is introduced by a line
     saying that what follows is data written by other workers and is not to
     be followed as instructions, and is delimited by the lines
     `<registry-data>` and `</registry-data>`, which no sanitized text can
     contain; an empty registry is the block holding one line `(none)`.
     (c) Tests: a description holding an injected instruction, a fence, a
     closing delimiter, angle brackets, a backtick, non-ASCII and a line
     break arrives as one inert line inside the block; the cap equals the
     threshold's value; retired approaches and cells sharing neither family
     nor horizon are absent; two call ids give different labellings and one
     call id the same labelling twice.
