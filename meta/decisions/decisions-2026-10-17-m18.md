142. **DESIGN §18.3 G3.3, §13.1, §13.3, §13.4; finding D-017; REVIEW-17
     blocker 1: what G3.3's "no raw bar" and "no score" assert.** This
     replaces decision 139 (c)'s clause "no raw bar (...) and no score
     (...)"; the rest of 139 (c) stands. Searching the prompt's text for a
     bar's or a score's written forms cannot work alone: the prompt writes
     every float as `.4g` (decision 117), and on `test_synth` the profile's
     basis-point statistics meet SYNA's prices and the trial figures at
     four significant digits by chance (REVIEW-17 counted 23 prompts). The
     reading: a prompt holds a raw bar or a score when one of its numbers
     is one, or is computed so as to carry one; the harness builds a prompt
     only from named sources; so G3.3 asserts it in three parts, (a) to
     (c), each on every message of every stored response of the G3.1
     replay, and keeps the text search of (d).
     (a) Accounting. Every numeric token of every message (the tokens
     `m3_harness.numbers` finds) is accounted for by a source of the part
     it stands in (DESIGN §13.4's five parts, split at the headings
     `workers.prompt` writes), each source computed by the test for that
     call, day and cell; a token no source accounts for fails the item,
     whatever its value. The sources:
     - part 1, the brief: the integers of the cell line, from the track
       configuration (the horizon's bounds, the bar's seconds), and the
       tokens of the brief the coordinator gives for that day and cell
       (the scripted coordinator's template, filled by the test);
     - part 2, the W profile: the tokens of the introduction's fixed text;
       the tokens of the keys and of the integral leaves of the anonymized
       profile document (`anonymize.anonymize(profile.profile(...), call
       id)`); and, for each finite real leaf, `format(leaf, ".4g")`, the
       test writing that form itself;
     - part 3, the registry: the ids, cells and states of the approaches
       the registry lists for that day and cell, read by the test from the
       state's approaches, and the tokens of each one's stored description,
       each of which the test also finds in the corpus reply that approach
       was admitted from (worker text, not harness text);
     - parts 4 and 5, the contract and the output format: the tokens of the
       two parts with the cost lines taken out, a text the test asserts is
       identical in every first prompt of the replay (it holds the caps,
       the indicator signatures and the contract's constants, and nothing
       of the day), and, in each cost line, the four figures written as
       `.4g` of the per-unit cost means in basis points of that label's
       instrument over W (`prompt.unit_costs` may give them; (b) judges
       their values);
     - on attempt 2: the first two messages equal attempt 1's stored
       messages (accounted there); the assistant's message equals, byte for
       byte, the corpus file the cursor served (its tokens are the
       worker's); the retry message is the fixed retry text and the error
       text the test recomputes, as G3.2 does.
     (b) Values. Every harness-computed value (a) admits, each finite real
     leaf of the anonymized profile document and each cost-line figure, is
     compared at full precision (`math.isclose`, relative tolerance 1e-9)
     with every open, high, low and close of every bar of the day's W and T
     of every instrument, both as stored and multiplied by that
     instrument's scaling factor for the call (`anonymize.labelling`), and
     with every non-integral figure of every trial row dated before the day
     (the walk `figures` does over `metrics_w`, `metrics_t`, `adjusted_w`,
     `adjusted_t` and the grid's results). No value meets one. A meeting the
     test cannot explain by its source is not waved through: the unit
     stops as item 141 (a) says, naming both values.
     (c) Units. For three days of the replay written in the test (the
     first, the last and one between), the profile document computed from
     a copy of the state's bar store, written under the test's temporary
     directory, in which every open, high, low and close is multiplied by
     7/3 and every volume by 5/2 (and whatever else the store holds in
     price units, such as roll constants, scaled the same way, so that the
     copy is the same market in other units), equals the profile computed
     from the store: every finite leaf to a relative 1e-9 and every null in
     the same place (DESIGN §13.1: every field is free of price and volume
     units). This is what makes a bar carried through a computation, not
     only a bar copied, fail G3.3.
     (d) The text search of 139 (c) stays as written in
     `tests/gates/test_g3_3_prompts.py`, with `looked_for` unchanged: it
     looks for every `repr` and every form longer than `.4g`, and (a) to
     (c) now cover the `.4g` forms it skips. The module docstring states
     this item's reading in place of D-017's, and cites it.
     (e) Seen to fail, each made alone in a worktree and reverted, naming
     the part, (a), (b) or (c), and the test that went red: the last close
     of W of each instrument added as a field of the profile's common
     block; the same close multiplied by the call's factor added instead; a
     figure of the latest earlier trial row (its `metrics_w` Sharpe)
     appended to each registry line; one open of W written into the cost
     lines' text; a figure of an earlier trial row added as a field of the
     profile document. Each must turn G3.3 red.
     (f) G3.3 finishes within 15 minutes (DESIGN §18.2); the report gives
     its wall time beside REVIEW-17's 158.80 s.
     (g) Finding D-017 is answered by this item.
